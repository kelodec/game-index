Basic Fantasy RPG

A straightforward old-school fantasy roleplaying game focused on exploration, resourcefulness, and the choices players make.

What's it like?

Players create adventurers, venture into dungeons and wild places, and return with treasure and stories—if they survive. The rules are deliberately lean, leaving room for creative solutions and decisions that aren't limited to a character sheet.

Why I'm considering it

It's easy for someone to join a session without learning a complex ruleset. Its open-ended structure also makes it a natural candidate for an open-table campaign where the group can change from week to week.

At a glance

  • Genre: Classic fantasy
  • Style: Dungeon crawling and exploration
  • Rules: Lightweight, old-school
  • Experience: Especially suited to open-table play
