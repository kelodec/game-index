Dragonbane

A fast-paced fantasy roleplaying game about adventurers taking risks, exploring dangerous places, and occasionally getting in way over their heads.

What's it like?

Dragonbane combines classic fantasy adventuring with approachable rules. Characters explore, investigate, negotiate, and fight their way through a dangerous world. Combat is quick and risky, so clever plans can be just as valuable as strong weapons.

Why I'm considering it

It offers a satisfying middle ground: enough structure for interesting decisions, but not so many rules that a new player needs to study beforehand. It should work well for stand-alone adventures as well as ongoing campaigns.

At a glance

  • Genre: Fantasy adventure
  • Style: Exploration and tactical risk
  • Rules: Relatively streamlined
  • Experience: Stand-alone or campaign play
